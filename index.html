🎵 Get Deezer ARL Cookie

This tool helps you extract your Deezer ARL cookie value using a simple bookmarklet.

⚠️ Important: Your ARL cookie is like a password. It gives access to your Deezer account. Never share it with anyone you don't trust!

📚 Instructions

Step 1: Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:
Get Deezer ARL
Step 2: Go to deezer.com and log in to your account
Step 3: Click the bookmarklet you just added to your bookmarks bar
Step 4: Your ARL value will appear in a popup. Click "Copy to Clipboard" to copy it!

❓ Why a Bookmarklet?

Due to browser security (Same-Origin Policy), a regular webpage cannot access cookies from other domains like deezer.com. A bookmarklet is a small JavaScript snippet that runs on the Deezer page itself, so it can safely access the cookies.

🔧 Alternative: Manual Method

Open deezer.com and log in
Press F12 to open Developer Tools
Go to the Application tab (Chrome) or Storage tab (Firefox)
Click on Cookies → https://www.deezer.com
Find the cookie named arl and copy its value

💡 How It Works

The bookmarklet reads all cookies from the current page, finds the one named "arl", and displays it in a user-friendly popup with a copy button. It only works when you're on deezer.com because that's where the ARL cookie exists.
